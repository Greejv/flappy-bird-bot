ILLUSTRATION · controls

🟢 Mouse IN the game → bot plays

The bot clicks by itself (the cursor jumps into the game). Don't move the mouse while it plays.

🟠 Mouse OUT of the game → pause

Move the mouse out and the bot stops clicking. Move it back in to continue.

🔴 Kill instantly

esc
Works anywhere — even with the game's browser in front. Other key: --kill-key ctrl+alt+k

⌨️ Quit in Terminal

control+C
Click into the Terminal window and press Control + C.
